# Roadmap

Status as of November 2026.

1. ✅ **Foundation**: settings (Home Assistant address and token), local address first and Tailscale as fallback, connection status.
2. ✅ **Flow diagram**: animated dashboard of the heat pump (own graphics, not copied from Thermia).
3. ✅ **History**: charts with Swift Charts, heating curve analysis and degree minutes.
4. ✅ **Menus**: Information and Service, following the pump's own structure, read-only.
5. ✅ **Alarms**: active alarm and alarm history.
6. ✅ **Widget**: home screen and lock screen.
7. **Publish the source**: remove personal defaults, make the entity prefix a setting, rename internal types to OpenDCM.
8. **Language**: Swedish and English, selectable in settings.
9. **iPad layout.**
10. **Later, maybe**: writing settings. Not planned until the read-only app is solid.
